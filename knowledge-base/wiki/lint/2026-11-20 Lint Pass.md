# 2026-11-20 Lint Pass

## Scope

Forty-eighth pass, following the November 20 ingest of 30 sources. The starting inventory was
**742 typed wiki pages**. Adding this report leaves **743**, including **417 source summaries**,
**148 concepts**, **108 entities**, and **48 lint reports**, with **443 owned source IDs**.
Local-only query files are excluded.

Structural coverage repeated the standing audit: frontmatter and directory/type agreement,
source ownership, index/log/overview parity, wikilink resolution, reachability, index
categories, Related-pages endings, source-summary sections, source/post/query boundaries, and
affected-page/body-citation reciprocity. A supplementary survey checked canonical metadata across
all **449 top-level raw captures**. The publisher check compared each source summary's author
with the matching entity page in both directions. The five new concepts and two new entities were
compared with their nearest existing pages for near-duplicates. [[Low-Precision Training]] defers
deployment compression to [[Model Quantization and Efficiency]], and [[Neural Audio Codec]]
cross-links [[Neural Text-to-Speech]] and [[Real-Time Voice AI]] in both directions; none
duplicates an existing page.

Semantic review covered all 30 new source summaries, the seven new pages, and the consumer edits
the ingest made to existing pages, including the November 20 overview section and the tension
note added to [[ByteByteGo - Three LLM Search Architectures for Delivery Platforms]]. Six
read-only reviewers compared those pages with the raw captures. For the PDF-backed
[[Anthropic - The Founder's Playbook - Building an AI-Native Startup]], they used text extracted
from `startup_guide.pdf`. Every finding was then checked against the raw text before any edit.
The pass did **not** revalidate every older summary, reproduce benchmarks, execute captured code,
or fetch external papers.

The preservation baseline was the knowledge-base working tree after the finished ingest, not
Git HEAD.

## Findings

### The graph was clean; the claims were not

The structural audit returned zero findings before and after the fixes, apart from the deferred
off-domain capture `Post by @Tristan0x on X`, which stays uncited by design. All **743 pages**
are reachable afterward, index/log/overview parity holds at **443 IDs**, and affected-page
reciprocity is **A 0 / B 0**. Body-citation reciprocity is **C 0**: no concept, entity, or
synthesis cites a source summary in its body without that source's ID.

Semantic review confirmed **46 findings**: **15 errors**, **16 overclaims**, **4
misattributions**, and **11 missing qualifiers**. All were fixed. A propagation scan found one
copy beyond the reviewed lines: the "no lab has responded" wording on [[Open Model Ecosystems]],
corrected and counted under G3-6.

### Forensic readings were given wider reach than the report claims

- **Which rows are provisional:** [[Checkpoint Forensics]] placed the report's "one analysis
  pass, not yet re-run" mark among the six Blackwell-matching models. In the raw it marks two
  Hopper readings, Ling-1T and GLM-4.5, with their same-init siblings. The page now says none of
  the six carries the mark.
- **K3.1-Pro:** the summary and the concept said it rested only on sharing K3's seed. The report
  gives it its own statistical reading (**305 sigma**, same seed), still without an independent re-run.
- **Laptop reproduction:** "anyone with a laptop can re-run it" now applies to the published
  check for five readings, not the original search over roughly 6 trillion combinations.
- **Token counts and authorship:** DeepSeek's token counts are its own; only the others are the
  author's unpublished estimates. "Single analyst" became "single-author report". The overview
  and [[Open Model Ecosystems]] now say no lab response is reported, not that no lab responded.

### Results were stretched beyond their test conditions

- **Integer inference:** [[Inference Determinism]] and [[Multi-Turn Evaluation]] used the
  step-0 cushion (**0.094** drift against a **2.66** gap) as the reason all 512 tokens matched.
  The author calls that cushion unusually generous; **45** of the 512 steps had gaps under 0.5.
  The concept also restores "mostly" to reduction order versus thread races, and now says the
  float-to-int conversion ties the guarantee to one prepared artifact.
- **Compaction:** [[Alpha Signal - Why AI Agents Don't Always Need Long Contexts]] restores "at
  its best compaction setting", and separates the accuracy results, which used two hosted models,
  from the throughput results on self-hosted open-weight models. One of those collapsed under a
  16K limit.
- **Tool deferral:** [[Tool Roster Economics]] said OpenRouter's test answered no only for
  Haiku. Gemini's cost also rose (**35,409** to **37,568**). [[Agent Skill]] no longer says the
  skill "bought nothing"; it added tokens without a correctness gain for a well-known CLI.
- **Slop trends:** [[AI Code Review]] said giant functions fell across model families. The
  newer GPT runs got worse on them. [[AI-Native Software Development Lifecycle]] no longer
  claims LibraryUseBench solutions pass lint, typecheck, and tests; the post reports no such
  results.
- **Weight-space programming:** [[Neural Network Fundamentals]] said hand-written networks
  "matched or beat training" on compact-rule tasks. Only bracket matching has a trained
  comparison (**96.6%**). [[Weight-Space Programming]] no longer asserts that no hand-crafted
  pipeline exists for cats versus dogs.
- **Instacart:** the rewrite gain came from typed sub-tasks plus per-type prompts, examples,
  and post-processing filters, with no ablation. "1% milk" becoming "one percent milk" was the
  article's illustration, not an observed failure.
- **4-bit training:** the roughly 3x speedup was theoretical and limited to the MoE forward
  pass, and the **2%** compute-efficiency cost on [[Thinking Machines]] applies to pretraining.

### Wording drifted from the sources

- **Strauss** says an explainable network "may be worth more than a few points of accuracy";
  the summary and [[Mechanistic Interpretability]] had dropped "more than".
- **Toumbourou's** conclusion now carries his "from these experiments at least".
- **Nanda's** finding is an "increased ability" to answer without reasoning, not answering
  "more often"; the filler-token summary and [[Chain-of-Thought Monitoring]] now say so.
- **Kinference:** the benchmark ran on "relatively old" L40S GPUs, not "small" ones, and
  [[ML Systems at Scale]] now gives the two identical pools rather than "the same three
  replicas".
- **Antigravity:** [[KV Cache]] called the +4-minute keep-alive a 1-token background ping; it
  was an ordinary user turn. The overview credits the cache-expiry finding to per-turn token
  measurement, not to the binary reading.
- **Smaller corrections:** LiquidCodec's three seeds were generation seeds at evaluation, not
  training seeds. EP233's deep-research system "may" ask clarifying questions. The Voicebox
  session cookie comes from a one-time URL. ioma8's agents "give up" without the tooling skill;
  they do not misuse the tools. Kinlan's saved scripts run first and "can" skip the model.
  Bombadil runs until a property holds or fails. Shankar and Husain's stylist example keeps its
  "may" hedges, and Pueyo labels only one forward reference premium.

### Credit went to the wrong actor

- **TorchTPU:** [[Ebba Simpson - Introducing TorchTPU]] credited Ebba Simpson, a name that
  appears only in the capture's `author` field. The body credits "The TorchTPU Team", about 53
  contributors. The summary now credits the team and records the publishing-account pattern
  that the Helion post also shows. The page title and `source_id` keep the earlier name so that
  links and IDs stay stable.
- **Founder's Playbook:** the guide blames AI research tools, not agentic coding, for
  confirmation bias, and only several of its failure modes come from speed. [[Agent Skill]] no
  longer calls its examples instruction-only; the guide only names them.
- **Kinlan's extensions:** repeatability and untrusted-web handling are his own design choices,
  not requests from the teams he shared the runtime with.
- **Baseten:** the teardown's author claims no independent timing; the entity had said none
  exists.
- **Instacart relays:** three pages called both ByteByteGo articles relays of "the same
  Instacart post". The earlier article cites two Instacart posts, so the pages now name them.
  The index no longer states the 2%/6% conflation as settled.

### Publisher links are unchanged by design

The publisher check found **72** one-way pairs. **59** are [[AI Builder Club]] and **10** are
[[ByteByteGo]] summaries; both entities describe the publisher and its collections. The other
three are author affiliations or hosting platforms (Google Cloud, Mistral, GitHub) that are not
the publisher of the source. The new ByteByteGo pairs come from this week's ByteByteGo
captures and the Evals excerpt it published. No new pair needs a reciprocal link.

### Seventeen Related-pages links point to uncited summaries

Seventeen concept and entity pages link a source summary only from `## Related pages` without
carrying its ID, for example [[Model Routing]], [[Context Engineering]], and
[[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] to the Sarthak Rastogi production-readiness summary, and
[[Hamel Husain]] to the decision-models taxonomy. These are navigation links, not citations, so
they do not break reciprocity and were left in place.
