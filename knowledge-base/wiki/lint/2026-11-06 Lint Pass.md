# 2026-11-06 Lint Pass

## Scope

Forty-sixth pass, following the November 6 ingest of 21 sources. The starting inventory was
**668 typed wiki pages**. Adding this report leaves **669**, including **363 source summaries**,
**139 concepts**, **101 entities**, and **46 lint reports**, with **389 owned source IDs**.
Local-only query files are excluded.

Structural coverage repeated the standing audit: frontmatter and directory/type agreement,
source ownership, index/log/overview parity, wikilink resolution, reachability, index
categories, Related-pages endings, source-summary sections, source/post/query boundaries, and
affected-page/body-citation reciprocity. A supplementary survey checked line endings, wrapped
links, and canonical metadata across all **394 top-level raw captures**. A new publisher check
compared each source summary's author with the matching entity page in both directions.

Semantic review covered all 21 new source summaries and the consumer edits the ingest made to
existing concept, entity, and summary pages. Six read-only reviewers compared those pages with
the raw captures; every reported finding was then checked against the raw text before any edit.
The pass did **not** revalidate every older summary, reproduce benchmarks, execute captured code,
or fetch external papers.

The preservation baseline was the knowledge-base working tree after the finished ingest, not
Git HEAD, because outside auto-backup commits advance HEAD during work.

## Findings

The standing structural audit and the supplementary checks returned zero findings before and
after the fixes. All **669 pages** are reachable afterward, index/log/overview parity holds at
**389 IDs**, and reciprocity is **A 0 / B 0 / C 0**.

misattribution**, **14 overclaims**, and **22 missing qualifiers**. All were fixed.

### A citation the capture does contain was reported missing

[[axjns.dev - The Synthetic Membrane - A Coordination Layer for Multi-Agent AI Systems]] said
in-text citation **[13]** had no matching reference and the list ended at 12. The capture's
reference list contains entry 13, a FEMA ICS review document. The false bullet is removed and
the index catalog line no longer repeats it. The November 6 ingest log entry still records the
old claim; the log is append-only, so this pass's log entry records the correction instead.

The same chain turned a suggested first implementation into a requirement. The membrane source
offers transactional updates as one way to start, not as the only coordination mechanism.
[[Harness State Authority]], [[LLM Application Resilience]], and
[[Multi-Agent Coordination]] now keep "where policy permits", "may share", and the proposal's
own statement that none of its ideas is individually new.

### Charts were described as uncaptured when they survive as geometry

[[General Reasoning - How to Train a Swarm]] and four consumers said the swarm post's chart
values were not captured. The capture keeps the z-scored curves and the twelve per-benchmark
latency charts as SVG with some axis ticks, and the latency charts keep marker coordinates; the
EdgeBench chart keeps its series paths; only the useful-work chart is a bare title. No value is stated in text. The
pages now say exactly that and do not add estimates the post does not state.

Related corrections in the same chains:

- [[Agentic Reinforcement Learning]] said every method above the swarm section trains one agent,
  contradicting its own OpenAI incident paragraph, which records side effects of multi-agent
  training.
- [[Multi-Agent Coordination]] merged two different outcomes. Model mix shifted who led; name
  order shifted who issued plans.
- [[Agent Delegation]] now states Raindrop's leader rule rather than the vaguer "changed several
  teammates' decisions", and says self-reports alone cannot reliably audit intent drift.

### Boundary and sandbox claims were stated more strongly than the tests

[[Celln]] and [[axjns.dev - Testing Celln's Boundary]] reported four denials. The capture
reports four operations that were rejected **or unavailable**; one probe returned ENOENT
because the path was absent, which does not show that Landlock blocked it. The entity also
described five lifecycle stages that every program passes, when only a successful build
reaches a cell. The entity now says ordinary seccomp BPF does not compare a pathname with a
binary allowlist, and records the vsock broker as a possible, untested channel subject to host
policy.

Other G3 corrections restore the author's framing: the lease post's Sympozium is the author's
own coordination project; [[AI Agents in Production]] separates durable coordination from
transient execution rather than placement from coordination; [[Legacy Code Modernization with AI Agents]]
says crawler traffic **and logged-out pages** moved first. The Netflix summary and the
[[ByteByteGo]] entity now say the article does not state whether its code snippets are Netflix
code, and [[Software Performance Engineering]] calls scoping a key lesson, not the lesson.

### Measurement scope went missing in benchmark and training claims

- **Droid 1.0:** [[Factory AI]] now says cost and time count only tasks both harnesses passed
  in all three trials. The line-numbering change helped Sol and Opus and raised cost on GLM and
  Kimi; the summary had said it helped one model and hurt two. The 5-minute program
  wait returns partial output and can also start in the background.
- **Compute islands:** [[Distributed Training Parallelism]] separates DiLoCo's pseudo-gradient
  outer optimizer from the different DisTrO/DeMo mechanisms, and [[ML Systems at Scale]] gives
  the 72% figure as a historical rate.
- **Weight-space programming:** the Takehi summary now records that a re-run was acknowledged
  but its results are not reported in the capture.
- **Reasoning and attention:** [[LLM Reasoning]] and [[Qwen]] attribute the Astra result to its
  own report; [[KV Cache]] scopes the 32/2 head counts to the Lightning attention layers.
- **Product and model claims:** [[Figma]]'s 33% rating gain has no stated baseline;
  [[Tabular Foundation Models]] attributes Timestral's lead to Mistral; the offloaded-inference
  summary scopes its 45-second timeout to non-streaming endpoints.
- **Simulation:** [[Autonomous Driving Systems]] and [[NVIDIA]] say AlpaSim forwards to
  Sim-Auto, and the robotics checkpoints are described separately from the surgical pair.
- **Human deferral:** [[Human Deferral to AI]] restores the scope of the 71 versus 73 confidence
  comparison and the 61% always-guess baseline, says no measured characteristic predicted
  deferral after correction while keeping the low-power caveat, and hedges a closing inference.
- **Hedges:** [[Emergent Misalignment]], [[Sycophancy]], [[Agent Observability]], and
  [[Programmatic Tool Calling]] keep the sources' tentative wording and measurement detail.
