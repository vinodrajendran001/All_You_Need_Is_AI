### An implementation change was mistaken for feature chronology

[[Asaf Ben-Tovim - How AI21 Manages Its GPU Fleet with Kueue]] now says that AI21's revised
deployment **enabled TAS that was already available but unused in its v0.10 setup**. The previous
wording could be read as both TAS and Admission Fair Sharing first appearing in v0.15.
Admission fairness remains separate from preemption fairness, and the 72-to-12-hour result remains
a queue-wait improvement, not a training-throughput gain.

### A geometric intuition had become a mathematical proof

[[@docmilanfar - A Lagrangian View of Flow Matching]] and its consumers treated several stronger
claims as consequences of a transport identity:

- `partial_t f + J_f v = 0` does **not** force straight characteristics or `partial_t f = 0`.
  For a rotation, `f(x,t) = R(-t)x` is constant along a circular trajectory; its partial-time and
  transport terms cancel.
- Additive Gaussian noise does **not** imply exploding posterior covariance. With a unit Gaussian
  prior and noise scale `sigma`, posterior variance is `sigma^2/(1+sigma^2)` and the denoiser
  derivative is `1/(1+sigma^2)`. The variance shrinks and the derivative remains bounded.
- The source does not establish inevitable literal path intersections, zero uncertainty after
  reflow, capacity-independent quality, or a universal solver-step requirement.

Recorded these counterexamples in the source summary and corrected [[Flow Matching]],
[[Diffusion Models]], [[Knowledge Distillation]], [[Neural Text-to-Speech]], the author entity,
overview, and index. The original source remains unchanged, and its claims remain explicitly
attributed. The correction does not say flow matching or reflow is ineffective; it separates a
useful intuition from an invalid general implication.

### A subsystem speedup had lost its subsystem

[[Halo Research - Sopro V2 On-Device Text-to-Speech]] reports **32 to two acoustic solver steps**
and a **16x acoustic-head speedup**, not a 16x end-to-end TTS improvement. Its flow-matching head
produces mel spectrograms; Vocos is a separate vocoder. Repaired that distinction across the
speech, small-model, edge, and flow-matching pages.

Restored the latency setup: **single-stream PyTorch, default settings, no batching**; 0.24 RTF
is M3 offline, approximately 300 ms is M3 time to first audio, and 0.07 RTF is H100 offline.
Kept quality parity as the authors' reflow-stage claim. The published 0.5B/32-step versus
120M/two-step tables also change model size and training, so they are not a reflow-only ablation.

Replaced the inferred V1 vocabulary-budget percentage with the supported **49M embedding-parameter**
figure. The later 120M Turbo total is not a V1 denominator. [[Flow Matching]] now synthesizes this
bounded engineering report with the geometric source instead of relying on the latter alone.

### An inconclusive judge comparison had become a zero-effect result

The three Giles Thomas summaries were too compressed to support their consumers' conclusions.
Rebuilt them from the captures:

- Part 1 compares FineWeb loss with **GPT 5.5 grades after Alpaca instruction fine-tuning**.
  Grades out of 100 are not accuracy percentages. Its table is pre-fix, and OpenAI small's
  roughly 124M tied parameters are not matched to Thomas's roughly 163M untied models.
- Part 2 fixes a live-reference `state_dict()` snapshot and changes validation from five batches
  to the full set. Some dropout settings may also have differed. Those changes cannot all be
  attributed to deep copying, and the corrected baseline does not close the OpenAI gap.
- Part 3 compares one 6.4B-token epoch with two epochs over 3.2B tokens. First-judging gains are
  1.22 and 0.92 points; the two models reverse order in a second judging. The author's
  one-to-two-point rule is a heuristic, not a formal noise floor or proof of no benefit.

Corrected [[LLM Training Pipeline]], [[LLM-as-a-Judge]], and [[Benchmark Optimization]] accordingly.
Removed the claims that lower loss bought nothing and that the case established an uncontaminated
evaluation. The reported test loss was also used to select among checkpoints; it was not an
untouched final-only test. The author explicitly leaves the overtraining hypothesis unresolved.

### New qualifications had not reached older paragraphs

The overview and [[ByteByteGo]] entity still carried an unqualified RLHF-only sycophancy framing
alongside the newer correction. Aligned them with [[Sycophancy]]: the behavior predates RL,
preference learning can amplify it, and optimization effects depend on the setting.

Also narrowed the older RLHF/DPO summary's claim that imitation cannot teach a trade-off.
Standard SFT lacks an explicit pairwise preference term; that is not a proof that curated
demonstrations cannot encode preferences. Switching optimizers does not automatically repair
biased comparisons, but neither does shared data imply identical outcomes under all algorithms.
