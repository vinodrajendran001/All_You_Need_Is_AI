# 2026-09-30 Lint Pass

## Scope

Thirty-seventh comprehensive pass over **498 wiki pages**, **283 top-level raw captures**, and
**257 source summaries**, run immediately after the eleven-source September 30 ingest.

Two audits ran in parallel:

- A **structural** pass over orphans, thin pages, required sections, empty sections, duplicate and
  case-variant headings, single-source concepts, raw-capture hygiene, tag forks, stale `updated`
  dates, duplicate URLs and titles, index coverage, ambiguous page stems, and wikilink resolution.
- A **semantic** pass that traced all **eleven new evidence chains** end to end — raw capture to
  source summary to every citing page — looking for number drift, lost qualifying conditions,
  provenance promotion, silently resolved contradictions, and attribution errors. This is the audit
  the structural checks cannot perform, because every defect it found sits in prose that is
  well-formed, well-linked, and wrong.

## Findings

### Semantic: 39 findings across the eleven new chains, 10 of them high severity

The structural graph was almost clean; the evidence chains were not. The recurring failure is not
fabrication but **erosion** — a condition dropped, a hedge softened, an assumption hardened into a
measurement — each individually small and collectively enough to change what a claim means.

**Provenance promotion (highest severity).** Four places asserted as established fact what a source
offered as an assumption, a relative result, or a specification description:

- `Mixture of Experts` claimed MoE layers could be removed "**at no measured cost on two
  benchmarks**". The source publishes no unpruned scores at all; it claims only that its method
  *beats a block-influence baseline*. A relative win over a weaker baseline had been promoted to
  parity with the original model — and that promoted premise carried the page's entire redundancy
  argument.
- `Multi-Tenant Agent Architecture` said tenants are "**demonstrably** sharing cores". The 0.5
  cores-per-VM figure is the analyst's *assumed* base case inside an assumed 0.3–0.75 range. Nothing
  was measured. The preceding sentence correctly said "if that structure holds"; this one dropped
  the conditional.
- `Agent Payment Protocols` — a **new single-source concept page** — presented protocol spec text as
  established fact, with no Tensions section and no statement that its sole source is a secondary
  explainer, a grade two other pages already carried.
- The overview stated binary-reward "**training degenerates toward supervised fine-tuning**", which
  is empirical framing for what is a property of the *gradient*. It was the only Romero passage in
  the vault carrying no "pedagogical derivation" label.

**Statements that were simply false as written**, each replicated across several pages:

- "`min_latency = 0`" (3 pages) — in the source this is the *accumulator initializer* of a
  speed-of-light pseudocode loop, not a parameter or a vendor admission. On
  `Serving Benchmarks and Goodput` the fragment had been read as a vendor self-indictment.
- "no joins beyond (N>2)-way joins" (4 pages) — the source says it **never** does joins wider than
  two-way. The vault's phrasing asserted the opposite.
- `Group Relative Policy Optimization` said Romero named PPO/GRPO/DAPO "without characterising the
  later ones"; he characterises them explicitly as adding clipping, masking, or reweighting.
- The Kwok summary and `LLM-as-a-Judge` said the capture "**carries** no author at all". True at
  capture time, false after this vault normalized the capture during the same ingest — a provenance
  note invalidated by the vault's own action.

**Lost conditions that change what a number means:**

- The headline 81.6% / 87.6% verifier accuracies are **best-of-N selection** scores over candidates
  sampled by Opus 5 and Fable 5. The generator sets the ceiling. Neither model was named anywhere in
  the vault, so the figures read as properties of the verifier alone.
- Four Martin accuracy figures (74.4 / 87.8 / 88.9 / 98.9%) are **train-split** results. Only the
  held-out pair carried its condition, leaving readers two unexplained baselines — 74.4% and 78.6% —
  for the same configuration.
- GB200 specs were quoted without "**per GPU**", halving the named hardware, since the benchmark
  baseline is 16 GPUs (8 superchips), not 16 superchips.
- A replay ablation lost "for the same number of agentic steps", making a compute-matched comparison
  look unmatched.
- `Long-Horizon Credit Assignment` licensed subtracting "a baseline" without the token-independence
  condition that makes the licence valid.

**Evidence the vault held but suppressed, and hedges that went missing:**

- The MPP source reports **about 30,000 transactions as of August 2026** and calls the volume
  "relatively small". The figure appeared nowhere — while `Agent Payment Protocols` asked "Does the
  demand actually exist?", posing the question with the source's own answer withheld.
- A **categorical** "MPP has not defined a refund flow at all" had been narrowed on five pages to "no
  refund flow *for one-off charges*", implying session payments are refund-covered. They are not.
- "This is **one of** our biggest blind spots" became "**its** biggest blind spot" on six pages, and
  "the team's biggest unsolved problem" in one — two escalations of a source that ranks nothing.
- A "~200 employees" figure scoped by its source to *one* observation about Space setup had been
  attached to two other findings on five pages. `Agent Memory` stated "no denominator of eligible
  users" for the 64/14 counts, then supplied "~200 employees" twenty lines later — inviting a 64/200
  reading the source does not license, from populations that are not even the same (employees versus
  external users).
- A binary-reward estimator was described as an **average over the correct completions**; it is a
  **sum over correct completions divided by the full rollout count**. The two differ by the pass
  rate, so the effective step size shrinks as accuracy rises.
- A grader-**determinism** check (run the grader twice, report whether the verdict changed) was
  conflated on two pages with the **noise floor** (how far a score moves by chance). Running a grader
  twice does not measure that, and the noise floor is load-bearing for two stopping rules.
- A skill that **warns** at ~95% saturation was described as a **ceiling** at which hillclimbing "is
  abandoned"; nothing is abandoned automatically.

**A source-internal contradiction that had been silently resolved.** The Inferact post's lede claims
"roughly 1.4 to 2×"; its own chart reports 1.38× at batch 4 and 1.36× at batch 8. The vault had
quietly used the chart — more accurate, but the vault's rule is to *preserve* such contradictions,
not to arbitrate them invisibly.

### Structural: graph healthy, two real defects, one convention drift

- **0** orphan pages, **0** thin pages, **0** pages missing `## Related pages`, **0** broken
  wikilinks, **0** duplicate `source_url` or `source_title`, **0** ambiguous page stems, **0** pages
  with `updated` earlier than `created`, **0** namespaced tag forks at wiki level.
- **Three-way control parity holds at 283**: index, log, and overview agree with each other and with
  summary ownership exactly.
- **1 duplicate heading fork.** `Alpha Signal - The Return of Recursion` carried both
  `## Affected Pages` and `## Affected pages` with different contents, plus a title-case
  `## Key Claims`. The title-case block also listed the three control pages, which the source-summary
  template explicitly excludes.
- **15 empty `## Citations` sections** in source summaries — template residue that made pages look
  uncited.
- **Convention drift, not a broken chain.** 33 summaries lack `## Raw capture`, but **every one of
  them links its capture** under `## Citations`. Traceability is intact vault-wide. The real problem
  was upstream: the `source-summary` template documented only `## Citations`, while 224 of 257 pages
  use `## Raw capture` — so the schema and the practice disagreed, and new pages were correct only by
  imitation.
- **6 summaries have no `source_url`, and none is recoverable.** Notably the three Alpha Signal
  captures preserve only per-subscriber `app.alphasignal.ai/c?uid=…` tracking redirects, which are
  not citable URLs and should not be committed to a public repository; the others are an unattributed
  LinkedIn post (expiring CDN link only), an unattributed working note, and a community-compiled
  document.
- **The single non-date-prefixed raw capture is intentional.** `Cohere's North Mini Code Megakernel
  Serving Engine.md` is a *duplicate* capture, already labelled as such in its summary's Citations;
  its body is byte-identical to the primary `2026-09-08 …` capture apart from frontmatter tags.
- **The five `wiki/posts/*` drafts are reachable via `[[Post Archive]]`**, which the index does link.
  Not an orphan condition; recorded here so future passes stop re-flagging it.

## Fixes made

**Semantic — all 39 findings resolved** across roughly 45 pages, by four parallel agents partitioned
by file so no page had two writers, with the three control pages handled separately:

- Demoted the four promoted claims to what their sources support, and gave
  `Agent Payment Protocols` a new `## Tensions and limits` section recording its secondary-explainer
  grade, the absent refund flow, the small adoption volume, and that payment proves control of a key
  rather than customer identity.
- Deleted the `min_latency = 0` clause everywhere, replacing it on `Serving Benchmarks and Goodput`
  with the vendor's actual self-description ("errs on the side of over-estimating peak performance").
- Reversed the "(N>2)-way joins" phrasing to "joins are never more than two-way" on all four pages.
- Restored every lost condition: "per GPU" and "16 GPUs, not 16 superchips"; the Opus 5 / Fable 5
  candidate generators; the train/held-out split distinction on all four Martin pages; the
  compute-matched ablation clause; token-independence on the baseline licence; the word "processed"
  on the billion-tokens-per-minute figure.
- Restored the missing hedges and the suppressed 30,000-transaction figure; restated the refund
  claim categorically; de-escalated the blind-spot superlative on six pages; re-scoped "~200
  employees" to Space setup only and stated explicitly that the 64/14 counts are not a fraction of it.
- Corrected the binary-reward normalisation, separated the grader-determinism check from the noise
  floor, and restored the ~95% saturation warning as a warning.
- Preserved, rather than resolved, two contradictions: added the Inferact "1.4 to 2× versus its own
  1.36–1.38× chart" tension, and rewrote the GRPO baseline passage to record that leave-one-out
  adjudication is *this vault's* inference and that Romero's stated condition conflicts with his own
  endorsed estimator.
- Fixed attribution: the TypeSafe jaggedness notes (not Rastogi) document adversarial fragility, and
  the cookbook results (`jev-1.12`) and those notes (`jev-1.13`) describe **different model
  versions** — a split nothing previously recorded. Corrected the six-patterns enumeration, which had
  matched neither of the source's two lists. Restored `Christopher Ré`. Added `193.6x faster` to four
  enumerations that claimed completeness while dropping it.
- Updated `entities/Sarthak Rastogi`, which three pages cite for this material but which had never
  been given the new `source_ids` or the secondary-explainer grade.

**Structural:**

- Merged the duplicate `## Affected Pages` / `## Affected pages` fork and normalized `## Key Claims`
  on `Alpha Signal - The Return of Recursion`, dropping the control pages the template excludes.
- Populated **14** empty `## Citations` sections with the house `- Raw capture:` / `- Canonical URL:`
  pair recovered from each page's own frontmatter and raw link.
- Gave the **3** summaries whose URL is genuinely unrecoverable an explicit provenance note saying so
  and why, rather than leaving `source_url: ""` to read as an oversight. No URL was invented, and the
  Alpha Signal subscriber-tracking redirects were deliberately not recorded.
- **Updated the `source-summary` template** to document `## Raw capture` alongside `## Citations`,
  with guidance for both, closing the drift at its origin instead of rewriting 33 historical pages.
- Normalized the two full-vault-path wikilinks on the Dwarkesh flashcard summaries to the vault's
  short-stem convention, removing a recurring false positive from future passes.

**Verified after fixes:** 283/283/283/283 four-way parity, **0** broken wikilinks, **0** duplicate
headings, **0** empty sections, **0** summaries without a resolvable raw-capture link, and zero
residual occurrences of every corrected phrase.

## Follow-ups

- **47 legacy raw captures still lack `source_id`**, and **92 retain the un-normalized `clippings`
  tag** with **25** carrying `topic/*` namespaced tag forks. All are confined to the immutable raw
  store; the wiki layer is clean and every capture remains reachable with summary ownership. Recorded
  here so future passes treat these as known state rather than new findings.
- **6 summaries will never have a `source_url`.** Now documented in-page. Future passes should treat
  these as closed, not as missing metadata.
- **1 raw capture without a date prefix is an intentional documented duplicate.** Closed; do not
  re-flag.
- **22 single-source concepts**, now including [[Agent Payment Protocols]], which additionally rests
  on a *secondary explainer*. It is the weakest-supported concept page in the vault and should be
  revisited when a protocol audit or production measurement arrives.
- **Recorded nowhere, and worth a future ingest:** the MPP source's IETF standards-track submission,
  its Visa/Cloudflare-backed agent-identity layer, and its **dishonest-seller** guardrail — clients
  must verify amount, recipient, currency, and expiry from the *signed payload* and ignore the
  friendly text description. That last one is a live attack [[Agent Security and Governance]] wants.
- The largest accreted concept pages still need a dedicated editorial restructuring pass rather than
  automatic heading insertion.
- **Process note for future ingests.** Every semantic defect this pass found was introduced by the
  ingest that created the page, and none was visible to structural checking. The erosion pattern —
  conditions dropped, hedges softened, assumptions hardened — is systematic enough that chain
  tracing should run as part of ingest, not only at lint time.
