# 2026-10-23 Lint Pass 2

## Findings

### A text-memory example had become a universal rule

[[Agent Memory]] opened with "memory is explicit storage, not a hidden internal state,"
said the model "never accesses memory directly," and required retrieved memory to be a
"readable fact." Those statements generalized the pguso text-store design beyond its scope.
The same page already preserved a contrasting structural-memory view and now includes
[[Image-Aware AI Agent Memory - Oracle 26.8]], which stores original image bytes separately
from searchable descriptions.

The old wording also equated visibility into saved strings with full visibility into what
an agent "knows," and called loading all facts "exact-match retrieval." Loading a complete
store does not perform a matching step, and inspecting stored facts does not expose
parametric knowledge or prove access controls are correct.

**Fixed:** Scoped the application-mediated retrieval claims to the external-store example,
distinguished stored facts from model knowledge, described load-all accurately, and made
the broader requirement traceable evidence with controlled retrieval rather than plain
text. The structural-memory alternative and the original pguso example remain intact.

### New calibration evidence was incorrectly called the first

[[Typed Probabilistic Decision Models]] called Husain's results "the first third-party
probability-quality measurements this page holds for Jev." Earlier on the same page,
[[Siddhant Rai - Jev - Models Built for Decisions Rather Than Text]] already supplied
secondary accounts of third-party calibration studies: pooled Choice ECE **0.028**,
mean-dataset ECE **0.074**, and radiology recalibration **0.1235 to 0.0077**.
Rai's captured article also reports a radiology Brier score of **0.1269**.

**Fixed:** Described Husain as adding task-specific measurements alongside the earlier
secondhand studies, rather than asserting priority. His saved out-of-fold Brier figures,
private 98-trace task, and non-guaranteed recalibration result remain unchanged. Direct
experimental reporting and a secondary account are different evidence levels, not grounds
for pretending the older evidence was absent.

## Follow-ups

- **Review old absolutes when adding new evidence.** A correctly qualified new section can
  coexist with an inaccurate opening definition. Search for "always," "never," and "first,"
  then read their scope rather than automatically deleting them.
- **Coverage remains uneven:** 12 concepts still have a single owning source and eight
  entity main bodies are under 100 whitespace-delimited words. These are research/expansion
  candidates, not missing-link defects; no filler or unsupported evidence was added.
- **Nine summaries lack a citable URL.** The new image-memory clipping is one; its author
  and publication date also remain unresolved. Subscriber redirects are not replacements
  for canonical citations.
- **Legacy conventions remain non-blocking:** 24 older lint reports omit an empty
  `source_ids` field, 33 summaries use legacy raw-capture placement, and one historical
  log heading is duplicated. Post pages intentionally omit source ownership. Historical
  records were not rewritten merely to standardize formatting.
- Structural reciprocity checks prove that attribution records agree, not that the claim
  is true. Preserve the raw-to-summary-to-consumer reading step for future ingests.
