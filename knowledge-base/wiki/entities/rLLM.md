# rLLM

## What it is

rLLM is an open-source project and training framework for reinforcement learning with language-model agents.

## Why it matters here

Its real-time RL experiment gives the vault a concrete single-rollout continual-learning method, including batch-relative advantages, asynchronous policy staleness, PPO clipping, and truncated importance sampling across rollout and training engines.

## Notes

The reported MigrationBench setup mocks production-style traffic under clean verifiable rewards. It should be treated as evidence that the algorithm can work in a controlled stream, not as proof that unrestricted online learning is production-safe.
