# Himank Sehgal - Meta Research Scientist Interview Guide

## Summary

This community guide describes Meta Research Scientist interviews as recruiter and technical screens followed by coding, research, ML design, domain depth, and behavioral rounds. It emphasizes converting research experience into explicit choices, experiments, impact, and cross-functional execution.

## Key claims

- Coding tests practical implementation under time constraints.
- Research rounds probe ownership, novelty, experimental rigor, and lessons from failures.
- ML design requires metrics, data strategy, modeling choices, deployment constraints, and iteration.
- Behavioral preparation should cover ambiguity, collaboration, conflict, influence, and impact.

## Why it matters

The guide complements [[ML Research Interview Preparation]] with a product-research profile where publication depth and applied system reasoning are assessed together.

## Tensions / open questions

- The guide is unofficial and likely varies across organizations and hiring cycles.
- Reported questions should be treated as patterns, not a fixed question bank.
