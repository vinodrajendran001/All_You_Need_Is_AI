## Findings

### Fixed in this pass

1. **One self-reported architecture was stated too directly.**
   [[Nandakishor M - Non-Autoregressive Decision Models and Laya]] described Laya as an open
   421M-parameter model and its training method as a technical contribution in the narrator's voice.
   The page now says the author *presents* the system that way and *claims* reinforcement learning
   intended to improve calibration.
2. **One sentence conflated calibration with selective automation.** The summary said that a
   calibrated system needs an escalation action. Calibration concerns correspondence between
   probabilities and outcomes; escalation is a policy choice that uses confidence for selective
   automation. The corrected sentence says a system intended to automate selectively can use an
   escalation action in addition to a label.
3. **The architecture decomposition is now exact downstream.**
   [[Typed Probabilistic Decision Models]] no longer calls Laya a “421M bidirectional model.” It
   records a reported 421M total architecture containing a 395M bidirectional ModernBERT-large
   encoder, preserving the distinction between the whole system and its backbone.
