## Findings

### Fixed in this pass

1. **One secondary source summary silently resolved a contradiction in its capture.**
   [[Alpha Signal - How DeepSeek Made a Bigger Model Cheaper to Run]] said that the 552B backbone has
   384 routed experts, one shared expert, and six *routed* experts per token. The capture actually
   says "one shared, 384 routed with six shared experts per token." The final phrase conflicts with
   the one-shared-expert count, so changing "shared" to "routed" created an unsupported correction.
2. **The summary now preserves the uncertainty instead of guessing.** It records the 384-routed and
   one-shared counts, quotes the contradictory six-shared wording, and requires verification against
   DeepSeek's primary technical report before assigning the per-token experts. No downstream page
   repeated the disputed six-expert detail, so no propagated repair was necessary.
3. **The URL is not a second defect.** The captured Alpha Signal tracking link resolves to
   DeepSeek's announcement, which is why the canonical URL points there while the captured body and
   author remain Alpha Signal. The source page already labels the article a secondary explainer and
   records that provenance boundary.
