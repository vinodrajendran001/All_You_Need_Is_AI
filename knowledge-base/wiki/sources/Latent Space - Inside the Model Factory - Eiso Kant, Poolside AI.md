# Latent Space - Inside the Model Factory - Eiso Kant, Poolside AI

## Summary

This interview frames Poolside's advantage as a model factory: reproducible data and code, streamed mixtures, rapid experiments, and a release process that turns many training decisions into an operational system. It is a case study in research throughput rather than a claim that automation replaces research judgment.

## Key claims

- The interview reports 10,000-20,000 experiments per month with fewer than 70 researchers and five- to eight-week releases; these are company-reported figures.
- Laguna S is described as an MoE with 118B total and 8B active parameters, with up to 1M context.
- Agent assistance can accelerate experiment execution, but humans still direct research and promotion.
