# @0xCodez

## What it is

An X account that publishes long-form, numbered "roadmap" courses on building agent systems with
Anthropic's Claude models and Claude Code, and promotes the movez.substack.com newsletter. The vault
has two of its courses, both captured through an AI newsletter.

## Why it matters here

The two courses are practical syntheses of Claude Code features that the vault otherwise records only
in passing. [[@0xCodez - Graph Engineering with Claude - 14-Step Roadmap from 0 to Graph Architect]]
teaches dynamic workflows as graph design — edges only where one step reads another's output, code
edges and router decisions, verifier patterns, loop-until-dry cycles, and `pipeline()` as the default —
and informs [[Graph Engineering]], [[Agent Planning]], and [[Loop Engineering]].
[[@0xCodez - Build a Self-Improving Agent System with Fable 5 in 14 Steps]] assembles /goal,
Outcomes, Routines, a `STATE.md` file, and Skills into a frozen-weight improvement loop, informing
[[Loop Engineering]], [[Agent Memory]], [[Agent Skill]], and [[Continual Learning for Agents]].

The evidence grade is the reason to keep an entity page. Both courses are secondary and promotional:
they relay Anthropic experiments, quotes, and launch dates without links to the reports, use
rhetorical statistics ("9 out of 10 users", "9/10 notice"), and include headline claims their own text
does not support, such as coordination costing "zero model tokens" and a conclusion that calls a
Fable 5 versus Opus 4.7 comparison one where "The model is the same". Their mechanisms are useful;
their numbers should be traced to primary sources before reuse.

## Notes

- The Fable 5 course gives the model's price as "~5×" Opus 4.8 per token, which conflicts with the
  vault's other Fable 5 guide, which lists twice the price.
- Neither course reports a measurement of its own.
