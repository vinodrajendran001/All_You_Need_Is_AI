# 2026-10-09 Lint Pass

## Scope

Thirty-ninth comprehensive structural pass. The starting inventory was **524 typed wiki pages**.
Adding this report leaves **525**, including **275 source summaries**, **120 concepts**,
**77 entities**, and **39 lint reports**. The evidence inventory remains **301 owned source IDs**
and **301 top-level raw captures**; some summaries own more than one source.

Structural coverage includes frontmatter, dates, unique source ownership, exact control parity,
section order, empty/duplicate headings, internal-link resolution, index categories, reachability,
affected-page reciprocity, and source/post/private-query boundaries. This pass extends the checker
to recognize bare page titles and duplicate destinations inside Related pages lists.

Semantic review traced all **three October 9 source chains** through their captures and material
consumers: nine GenRec destinations, four decision-model destinations, and eight agent-stack
destinations. Older training, reasoning, harness, and inference claims were then reviewed where
they contradicted those updates or the previous lint's qualifications. This is not an exhaustive
fact-check of every sentence, a live check of every external URL, or a reproduction of published
experiments.

## Findings

### Related-page lists contained invisible and repeated edges

The previous structural checks resolved existing wikilinks but did not detect titles that were
never linked in the first place. The expanded check found **37 bare titles** and **nine duplicate
destinations across 17 pages**. Most bare entries came from the older Wafer integration.

Converted the 37 titles to wikilinks and removed the nine redundant list entries, including two
bare/link pairs that became identical after normalization. Retained distinct destinations and
section order; no artificial source attribution was added.

### Catalog descriptions still advertised superseded claims

The index continued to present straight flow paths as a PDE consequence, reflow as uncertainty
elimination, and SFT as unable to learn trade-offs, despite qualifications already recorded in the
corresponding summaries. Corrected those descriptions and restored **Sopro V2 Turbo's** reported
**120M, M3 offline, single-stream PyTorch, default-settings, no-batching** boundary for 0.24 RTF.
Its acoustic-head saving remains separate from end-to-end TTS performance.

The reasoning, DharmaOCR, and Nithin catalog descriptions now carry the qualifications developed
below. Historical lint reports and log entries were not rewritten to pretend these remaining
descriptions had been corrected earlier.

### A useful DPO result had become an impossibility claim about SFT

[[Dharma-AI - Direct Preference Optimization Beyond Chatbots]] reports **59.4% average relative
degeneration reduction after DPO versus SFT**, with **87.6%** best case, across five OCR families.
It also reports that SFT already reduced degeneration in four families.

The capture first calls loss granularity a conjecture and explicitly disclaims causal proof,
then later calls an anomalous result confirmation of the mechanism. The old summary retained only
the stronger interpretation. Recorded that internal tension and removed unsupported claims that
SFT and DPO affect orthogonal capabilities or that no amount of SFT could address the failure.

[[Direct Preference Optimization]] now distinguishes **reference-sequence likelihood** from
**explicit chosen/rejected comparisons**. Token-factorized likelihood conditions on the prefix;
it is not independent token classification. The DPO derivation's closed-form reward/policy
relationship also does not give model parameters in closed form from preference data.

Updated [[LLM Training Pipeline]] and the overview accordingly. Preference learning is a direct
comparison signal, not the only possible way demonstrations can encode a trade-off. A separate
reward model is not mandatory for every preference-based method. Preserved the reported OCR
results without treating them as a matched ablation of all alternative training strategies.

### A reasoning slogan blurred inference and training

[[Akhil Arora et al - Current Advances in LLM Reasoning]] contrasts imitation with reward-guided
exploration and reports Logic-RL transfer from puzzles to math. Its "SFT reproduces, RL discovers"
framing does not establish a universal SFT generalization limit or guaranteed RL discovery.
The deck itself leaves creation versus amplification of reasoning open.

Corrected its summary, [[LLM Reasoning]], [[Reinforcement Learning]], and [[LLM Training Pipeline]].
Fixed-weight inference-time search is distinct from SFT/RL updates to weights; the former does not
prove that all post-training merely reveals pre-existing skills.

### Verification controls had become proof of cause and safety

[[Alpha Signal - Why self-improving harnesses are the next frontier]] attributes results to
regression tests and structured search, but supplies no gate-only ablation or broad safety
evaluation. [[Agent Skill]] had strengthened that into "precisely because," while
[[Coding Agent Harness]] implied safe self-rewriting followed from the gates.

Replaced those causal and safety guarantees with the actual reported controls and their limits.
Kept the older **33-60% Terminal-Bench-2.0** and **33% to 47% GAIA** figures within that briefing,
without combining them with the newer Self-Harness result. Harness-only characterization now
belongs to the described workflow, not every variant of the HarnessX name.

### Common inference regimes had become universal rules

[[Nithin - What Actually Happens During LLM Inference]], the two related ByteByteGo inference
summaries, and their consumers now distinguish low-batch dense decode from batched, sparse, or
different-attention workloads. Prefill is not invariably compute-bound, decode is not invariably
bandwidth-bound, and a phase label alone does not determine a hardware purchase or speedup.

[[Arithmetic Intensity and the Roofline Model]] now describes a performance ceiling, not a
guarantee of achieved utilization. Restored the **QK/PV attention-core** boundary in
[[Changyi Yang - Why MLA and MTP Fight Each Other]], [[KV Cache]], and the roofline concept:
unchanged counted attention FLOPs do not mean unchanged total prefill cost.

Also corrected the connected measurement boundaries:

- `mmap` defers loading; it does not remove cold-page I/O or GPU-transfer cost.
- The 70B/16-bit example is roughly **140 GB of dense weights**, not universal per-device,
  per-token traffic after batching or sharding.
- The quoted **90-95% versus 20-40% utilization** figures lack a complete measurement setup.
- Continuous batching avoids whole-batch barriers, not all resource queueing.
- Disaggregation is conditional on the workload and transfer cost, not always superior to
  co-location.
- [[Philip Kiely - The Efficient Frontier of LLM Inference]] says gains **often** compound.
  Its two doublings giving 4x are conditional arithmetic, not a measured combined speedup.
- For `N >= 1` output tokens, TTFT already includes the first token; using mean subsequent
  inter-token time gives `TTFT + (N - 1) * TPOT`.

These qualifications now appear in the relevant summaries, inference concepts, overview, and
catalog instead of only in a late caveat.

### The newest evidence retained its important boundaries

[[ByteByteGo - How Netflix Taught an LLM to Recommend Movies]] and its consumers retain the
finite-catalog output interface, unspecified policy optimizer, and distinction between score
normalization and calibration. The **0.115%** and **0.006%** improvements remain printed
percentages in unnamed metrics from the reported four-week, approximately 10%-traffic test.

[[Swapnil Talekar - The Decision Model Gold Rush]] retains secondary attribution for DecideBench,
the **35-59% encoder-group range** rather than a Laya-specific score, distinct task/token price
units, and the difference between Choice concentration and event probability.

[[Ben Dickson - How AI Agents Are Learning to Optimize Their Own Stack]] retains the public-page
qualification: Qwen3.5-35B-A3B on AppWorld rose **22.5% to 52.2%**, which is **29.7 percentage
points** or **132% relative**. EnvHarness's fewer interaction steps are not a measured equivalent
compute reduction. WikiSkill and Raven have no isolated numerical result in the clipping.
