# 2026-08-25 Lint Pass

## Scope

Full audit of 326 tracked wiki pages (177 source summaries, 77 concepts, 42 entities, 27 lint reports including this one, 1 overview, 1 synthesis, 1 index, 1 log) plus the 204 raw captures and 13 assets. Run immediately after the August 25 ingest.

Checks: frontmatter completeness and type/folder agreement, tag-vocabulary consistency, `## Related pages` presence, broken wikilinks, orphans, index coverage, duplicate source IDs, control-page ID agreement, raw-to-wiki traceability, stale `updated` fields relative to cited sources, reciprocal linking, thin pages, and the query-privacy invariant.

## Findings

This pass found no broken links, no orphans, no missing pages, and no contradictions. Every defect was a **consistency or traceability** problem rather than a content problem — which is the expected failure mode now that the vault is past 300 pages and several ingests deep.

### 1. The 63-lesson synthesis could not be traversed to its own sources

[[AI Builder Club - Build AI Agents]] declared all 63 lesson `source_ids` in frontmatter and summarized them across six clusters, but contained **zero wikilinks** to any of the 63 source pages. The synthesis was therefore a dead end: a reader could not move from the argument to the evidence. This also left 50 of the lesson pages with only a single inbound link (the index), making them effectively invisible to graph navigation.

This was the most consequential finding of the pass.

### 2. Tag vocabulary had forked

The marker tag for source summaries had split three ways against the template:

| Tag | Pages | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `source/summary` | 107 | canonical per `templates/source-summary.md` |
| `source-summary` | 67 | drift |
| `source` | 3 | drift |

Because `source/summary` is a nested Obsidian tag and `source-summary` is flat, the two never unify in the tag pane — so any tag-based filter over source summaries silently returned an incomplete set. Two source pages carried no marker tag at all.

A second fork existed on topic tags: `ai-agents` (68 pages) and `agents` (53 pages) were used interchangeably for the same subject.

The drift was invisible to earlier passes because 17 pages use inline flow-style YAML (`tags: [a, b]`) rather than block style, so a block-style scan under-reports it.

### 3. Raw captures declared source IDs that no wiki page claimed

Eight ByteByteGo May-2026 raw captures carried `source_id`s that appeared in no wiki page's frontmatter. The articles *were* summarized — in the composite page [[ByteByteGo - System Design and AI at Scale (May 2026 Batch)]], which has a per-article section for each — but the composite declared only its own batch ID. The raw-to-wiki chain therefore broke on ID lookup even though the prose coverage existed. Previous passes recorded this as an accepted composite-capture limitation; it is fixable and has now been fixed.

### 4. Nine source summaries had no pointer back to their raw capture

The schema expects source pages to point back to the raw capture. Nine did not, despite all nine having an identifiable capture on disk. They clustered with the vault's shortest summaries, suggesting they were written quickly from short sources (two tweets, a three-part blog series, two newsletter items, an arXiv survey).

### 5. One raw capture violated the naming convention

`2026-08-05 AI Builder Club - MarkItDown … [2026 Guide].md` was the only capture in the vault using square brackets. Because `[` and `]` terminate Obsidian wikilink syntax, its source page could not use a normal `[[wikilink]]` citation and had fallen back to a URL-encoded relative Markdown link — the only such citation among 177 source pages.

### 6. Minor: `source_url` absence was represented two ways

Six source pages legitimately have no canonical URL (newsletter emails, an unattributed LinkedIn post, a community-compiled document, the unattributed Grok Bot note). Three expressed this as `null`, three as `""`, and one as a bare empty key.

### 7. Observations recorded but deliberately not "fixed"

- **310 non-reciprocal `## Related pages` edges.** These are overwhelmingly hub pages such as [[AI Knowledge Base Overview]], [[Agentic Loop]], and [[LLM Inference]] receiving links they do not return. Full reciprocity would bloat hub pages without aiding navigation. Not a defect.
- **Unlinked prose mentions of entities**, chiefly `AI Builder Club` (67 pages), `Anthropic` (15), and `OpenAI` (14). Most are incidental mentions where a link would be noise; only genuine definitional mentions were linked.
- **49 legacy raw captures lack a `source_id` and 5 lack frontmatter entirely.** These predate the schema. `CLAUDE.md` treats raw captures as immutable absent an explicit user request, so they were left alone. They remain covered by wiki summaries via title, not ID.
- **Five short source summaries** (the Giles Thomas three-part series, `@waterloo_intern`, `@neural_avb`) sit well below the 2,736-character median. They are short because their sources are short, and they carry correct caveats and links. Padding them would add words, not knowledge.

## Fixes made

- **Made the 63-lesson synthesis traversable.** Added all 63 lesson wikilinks to [[AI Builder Club - Build AI Agents]], distributed across its six existing curriculum clusters by subject rather than by capture order: 15 fundamentals, 14 tools/context/skills/memory, 6 harness, 10 loop, 9 graph, 9 evaluation/security/governance. Verified each lesson appears exactly once and all 63 resolve.
- **Unified the tag vocabulary.** Normalized 69 source pages to the canonical `source/summary` marker (53 block-style, 14 inline-style) and added the marker to the 2 pages missing it — all 177 source summaries now agree. Merged `agents` into `ai-agents` across 45 pages, including 8 concept and entity pages using inline YAML.
- **Closed the raw-to-wiki ID gap.** Declared the 8 constituent article IDs in the frontmatter of [[ByteByteGo - System Design and AI at Scale (May 2026 Batch)]]. Every raw `source_id` in the vault is now claimed by a wiki page — verified at 150 of 150.
- **Added `## Raw capture` sections to 9 source summaries**, confirming each pairing by matching the raw capture's `source:` URL against the summary's `source_url` rather than by filename similarity.
- **Renamed the bracketed raw capture** to `(2026 Guide)` via `git mv` to match both the convention and its own source page title, then converted its citation to a standard wikilink. Content was not touched.
- **Normalized `source_url` absence** to `""` on the three `null` pages and the one bare key.
- **Linked definitional entity mentions** on [[Model Context Protocol]] and [[Tool Use and Function Calling]], where [[Anthropic]] and [[OpenAI]] are named as the originators of MCP and function calling.
- Bumped `updated` on every page changed.

## Verification

- 0 frontmatter defects: every page has `type`, `created`, `updated`, `tags`, and `status`; every source page has `source_id`, `source_title`, a `source_url` key, and the `source/summary` marker.
- 0 duplicate source IDs; 0 summary IDs unregistered.
- Index, log, and overview hold an identical **194** unique source IDs.
