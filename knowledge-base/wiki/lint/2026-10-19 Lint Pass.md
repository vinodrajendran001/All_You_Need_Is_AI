# 2026-10-19 Lint Pass

## Scope

Forty-first comprehensive structural pass. The starting inventory was **556 typed wiki pages**,
counted from the working tree, excluding git-ignored local query files. Adding this report leaves
**557**, including **291 source summaries**, **126 concepts**, **82 entities**, and **41 lint
reports**. Evidence ownership is **317 source IDs**. The raw collection contains
**319 top-level captures** and **388 files**, not counting the four `raw/sources/.obsidian` settings
files.

Structural coverage includes:

- frontmatter schema, dates, and directory/type agreement;
- source ownership and exact index/log/overview parity;
- link resolution, including ambiguous attachment names, and reachability from the index;
- index categories, raw pointers, uncited captures, and duplicate source identities;
- forward and reverse affected-page reciprocity;
- frontmatter citations without a body link;
- Related-pages endings, duplicate or plain-text related entries, and empty or duplicate headings;
- tags, log heading format, and the source/post/private-query boundaries.

The semantic review covered three areas:

- Every numeric token the October 19 ingest added (**386**) was matched against the raw captures
  of the sources cited in the same section.
- The highest-risk passages were read against the captured text: claims about other labs'
  interpretability work, policy-gradient mathematics, serving benchmarks, and chunking inferences.
- Seven disproportionately thin summaries were rewritten from their full captures, and the concept
  pages that consume them were checked against the same passages.

This is not an exhaustive fact-check of every sentence, a live check of external URLs, or a
reproduction of any reported experiment.

## Findings

### The October 19 ingest left one control behind

The log's frontmatter `source_ids` ledger still held **309 IDs** and an October 16 date after the
ingest added eight sources to the index and overview. Added the eight IDs and updated the date.
All three controls now match the **317-ID** ownership set exactly.

### The newest chains are numerically traceable, with three repairs

Of the 386 numeric tokens, three were flagged. Two were attribution misses by the checker: the
Goodfire figures sit in paragraphs that credit the source by author name. The third was a real
ambiguity. [[Agent Observability]] presented the agent-runtime study's roughly 70% human-detection
share inside a paragraph about the clinical post, so it read as clinical evidence. Reworded to
attribute it to the runtime study.

Claim-level review found two qualifier drifts in the Goodfire chain:

- [[Transformer Architecture]] said SwiGLU's double-negative regime lets a neuron "fire for two
  unrelated reasons". The capture describes a sign-flipped echo that usually looks like the
  negative pole. Corrected.
- [[Mixture of Experts]] reported the Poe-neuron ablation (**87% to 50%**) without the capture's
  control: removing only the neuron's direct output contribution leaves **85%**, so most of the
  effect runs through the rest of the network. Added.

frontmatter, raw pointer, and Affected pages unchanged. They now run **705-1,050 words**:

before editing:

## Fixes made

Modified **49 existing wiki files** and added this report. The files are **28 source summaries**
(21 for reciprocity and seven expansions), **18 concepts**, and the index, overview, and log.
Existing source ownership and historical evidence were preserved; no source ID was added or
removed.

The final structural pass resolves all **557 typed wiki pages** from the index. All three
controls match the exact **317-ID ownership set**. Every source summary has a resolvable raw
pointer, and no top-level capture is uncited. There are no broken or ambiguous links, no duplicate
or plain-text Related entries, and no boundary violations.

reports are byte-identical to the fresh lint baseline, as are **387 of 388** raw files.
[[LLM-as-Judge When It Works, and When It Misleads]] changed line endings only during the pass. A
process outside this lint rewrote it at 16:36 from its CRLF working copy back to the committed
LF version, which it now matches exactly. Its content is unchanged, and this lint did not write to
it. The log body is unchanged except for its frontmatter ledger and the one appended lint entry.
