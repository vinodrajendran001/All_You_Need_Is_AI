# Himank Sehgal - Amazon Applied Scientist Interview Guide

## Summary

Himank Sehgal's community guide describes Amazon Applied Scientist interviews as a combination of recruiter screening, technical screening, and a five-round loop covering coding, ML breadth and depth, science application, and behavioral evidence.

## Key claims

- Coding emphasizes data structures, algorithms, and executable solutions.
- ML rounds test both mathematical foundations and deep knowledge of the candidate's specialization.
- Science application combines system design, metrics, tradeoffs, and product constraints.
- Leadership Principles and STAR stories are evaluated throughout the loop.

## Why it matters

The guide adds company-specific evidence to [[ML Research Interview Preparation]] and reinforces preparation across theory, coding, system design, and behavioral narratives.

## Tensions / open questions

- The guide is unofficial, role- and team-dependent, and may become stale.
- Community reports are subject to selection and recall bias.
