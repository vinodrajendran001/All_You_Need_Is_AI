# Himank Sehgal - Navi AI Scientist Interview Guide

## Summary

This community guide describes Navi's AI Scientist process as a screening round followed by five onsite rounds. The framing emphasizes applied execution in an AI-first fintech environment and warns that the process can be broad and unpredictable.

## Key claims

- The process tests coding and practical machine-learning problem solving across a long onsite loop.
- Preparation should connect modeling choices to product constraints and deployment rather than focus only on theory.
- The company-specific framing rewards speed and execution, but candidates still need breadth for unpredictable questioning.
- Completing every round does not guarantee an offer, according to the guide.

## Why it matters

The guide complements [[ML Research Interview Preparation]] with a product-oriented AI-scientist profile where execution speed, system reasoning, and breadth matter together.

## Tensions / open questions

- The guide is unofficial and likely varies across teams, levels, and hiring cycles.
- Reported questions should be treated as patterns, not a fixed question bank.
