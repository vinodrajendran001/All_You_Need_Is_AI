# Yifan Zhang

## Who they are

Yifan Zhang is the author of the Recurrent Looped Transformer technical report and implementation.

## Why they matter here

[[Yifan Zhang - Recurrent Looped Transformer]] connects three topics usually documented separately:
recurrent latent computation, persistent serving state across prefill and decode, and exact
current-policy replay for reinforcement learning. The source is a design specification rather than a
performance result, and its claims should remain at that level until training and benchmark evidence
is available.
