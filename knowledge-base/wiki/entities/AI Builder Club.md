# AI Builder Club

AI Builder Club is an educational publisher focused on practical AI-agent and coding-agent engineering. Its 63-lesson **Build AI Agents** collection spans first-principles agent loops, tool use, memory, MCP, context and harness design, loop and graph orchestration, evaluation, security, governance, and applied operating loops.

## Why it matters to this vault

The collection is useful less as 63 independent tutorials than as a layered curriculum. It connects the vault's existing agent concepts into a progression from **prompt → context → harness → loop → graph**, while repeatedly arguing that verification, permissions, observability, and ownership—not additional model calls—set the practical ceiling on autonomy.

Several articles synthesize practitioner posts, product documentation, or time-sensitive model claims. Their source pages preserve those claims with explicit uncertainty rather than treating the collection as primary evidence for every quoted benchmark, product, or attribution.
