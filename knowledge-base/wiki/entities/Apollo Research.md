# Apollo Research

## What it is

The research organization publishing the vault's proposal for embedded evaluations of
frontier-model scheming and loss-of-control risk. The captured article is institutionally
attributed: no individual byline was recovered.

## Why it matters here

[[Apollo Research - Principles for Embedded Evaluations]] moves evaluation independence
beyond model choice into access, funding, publication rights, and ongoing observation.
Its proposed assessments combine developer evidence, independent investigation, and a test
of whether the methods could have detected counterexamples.

This is the organizing source for [[Embedded Evaluation]]. Its five verdicts distinguish
adequate negative evidence, unresolved gaps, developer disclosure, evaluator discoveries,
and blocked access. They are assurance/incentive categories, not a ranking of incident
severity.

## Notes

- The public article establishes publication on **September 30, 2026**; the preserved
  October 12 capture lacks author and publication metadata.
- "Verified" remains subject to scientific uncertainty. A lack of access does not prove a
  particular unsafe behavior, and a self-reported incident can still be severe.
- Refuted claims remain in the record when a narrower post-remediation claim is assessed.
  This prevents an improved statement from erasing the failure of the original one.
- The article proposes eventual legislation and interim voluntary incentives. It is not
  evidence that this process is already legally required, completed at a named lab, or
  resistant to deliberate gaming.
