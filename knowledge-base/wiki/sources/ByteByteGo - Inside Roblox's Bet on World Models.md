# ByteByteGo - Inside Roblox's Bet on World Models

## Summary

Roblox's described approach is an engine-authoritative hybrid: deterministic game state, rules, and physics remain the shared-world substrate, while a learned video upsampler generates higher-fidelity visuals. The generated image is presentation, not the authoritative multiplayer state.

## Key claims

- Conditioning visual generation on engine state constrains the model with geometry and game rules rather than asking it to simulate the whole world.
- The article frames 2K/60fps delivery, long-context consistency, and multiplayer scale as targets/open work, not established shipped capability.
