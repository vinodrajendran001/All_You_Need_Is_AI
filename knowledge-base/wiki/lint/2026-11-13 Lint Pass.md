# 2026-11-13 Lint Pass

## Scope

Forty-seventh pass, following the November 13 ingest of 24 sources. The starting inventory was
**703 typed wiki pages**. Adding this report leaves **704**, including **387 source summaries**,
**143 concepts**, **106 entities**, and **47 lint reports**, with **413 owned source IDs**.
Local-only query files are excluded.

Structural coverage repeated the standing audit: frontmatter and directory/type agreement,
source ownership, index/log/overview parity, wikilink resolution, reachability, index
categories, Related-pages endings, source-summary sections, source/post/query boundaries, and
affected-page/body-citation reciprocity. A supplementary survey checked line endings, wrapped
links, and canonical metadata across all **419 top-level raw captures**. The publisher check
from [[2026-11-06 Lint Pass]] compared each source summary's author with the matching entity
page in both directions. The four new concepts and five new entities were also compared with
existing pages for near-duplicates; none were found.

Semantic review covered all 24 new source summaries and the consumer edits the ingest made to
existing concept, entity, and summary pages. Six read-only reviewers compared those pages with
the raw captures; every reported finding was then checked against the raw text before any edit.
The pass did **not** revalidate every older summary, reproduce benchmarks, execute captured code,
or fetch external papers.

The preservation baseline was the knowledge-base working tree after the finished ingest, not
Git HEAD, because outside auto-backup commits advance HEAD during work.

### Measurements were given wider scope than the sources stated

- **Neuron ablation:** [[Curt Tigges et al - Neurons as an Interpretable Basis]],
  [[Mechanistic Interpretability]], and [[Transformer Architecture]] gave the fall from
  **1,550** to **176** reasoning tokens as a general GSM8K effect. It comes from **24** problems
  selected because removing the regime curtailed their reasoning; all three pages now say so.
- **The Tornado neuron:** the **98.5%** place share holds over 100 million tokens of ordinary
  text. On a code corpus the capture reports **98%** of strong firings on Tornado or code that
  calls it. The summary and [[Mechanistic Interpretability]] now give both corpora.
- **Context-limit test:** the summary listed AUROC **0.927** and **0.978** under leakage checks,
  where they read as the judge's scores. They come from rerunning the model itself on the short
  snippet; the text judge sees only the snippet.
- **chrome-capture:** at 2160p, only the stop times (**91 ms** versus **20.5 s**) are
  resolution-specific; the roughly **10 s** saving is the whole benchmarked replay. The
  **59.8 Mbps** figure is not tied to a resolution, so [[Paul Irish - chrome-capture]] now sets it
  against both the **72 Mbps** 2160p120 and the **18 Mbps** 1080p120 targets.
- **Long-context training:** [[Distributed Training Parallelism]] merged two comparisons.
  Context parallelism doubles the length from about **53K** to **105K** tokens across two nodes;
  full recomputation then reaches about **820K**.
- **GR00T 2:** of the four human-demonstration tasks, three are chemistry-lab procedures and the
  fourth is conveyor package tossing. [[Robot Training Data]] and the GR00T 2 summary no longer
  call all four chemistry tasks, and the glove-and-camera description is scoped to the lab tasks.
  [[Group Relative Policy Optimization]] now says fixed-pipe fittings is the one task whose
  per-method numbers are given in prose, not the only task with numbers.
- **Arete:** the single Nous-built, single-judge caveat applies to the values measurements, not
  to Terminal-Bench-2.1. [[Open Model Ecosystems]] and [[Qwen]] now say so.

### Hedges and conditions were dropped

- **GitHub's Rust port:** the summary and [[Legacy Code Modernization with AI Agents]] restore
  "could have prevented some more" regressions, cancellation that did not **always** stop
  related work, and further gains that **could** come from redesign. The concept page also
  removes quotation marks from "translate first, redesign later", which is a paraphrase.
- **Code review:** [[AI Code Review]] restores the conditions on LinkedIn's zero-approval tier:
  trusted authors, passing security checks, and a second classifier that can still require a
  person. It also says Anthropic reports A/B testing its suppression filters, though not their
  cost.
- **Reasoning:** [[LLM Reasoning]] restores Wang's "can potentially generalize", and the Wang
  summary defines success probability over all paths, not only sampled ones.
- **Speculative decoding:** [[Speculative Decoding]] keeps the handbook's "may come from
  placement" rather than presenting placement as the traced cause.
- **Sparse attention:** [[Sparse Attention]] and [[Linear Attention and Recurrent Memory]] no
  longer say LSA and recurrent compression were combined; the capture tests native LSA inside a
  hybrid without naming the other layers.

### Credit went to the wrong actor

- The Instinct summary now says an early user found the undeletable Gmail records and GPCA's
  write-up reported it; [[Instinct]] keeps checkout, not delivery, as the step to verify.
- [[DeepSeek]] and the DoubleAI summary say DeepSeek predicted the probe's gain from held-out
  older problems; the probe did not predict its own gain.
- [[Automated AI Research]] credits the Neuron Scientist only with the **2,734** atlas pages,
  not with the low-effort labels for all neuron-poles.
- [[Test-Time Scaling]] marks the training-range ceiling as the vault's inference, not Wang's
  prediction.
- [[METR]] reports that Tillman calls the Codex-Max compaction evidence ambiguous rather than
  treating it as evidence against his own claim.
- [[Ethan Mollick]] no longer quotes a paraphrase. It quotes "there has not been formal
  acceptance yet" and adds his note that the Clay Institute appears to consider the result
  settled.

### Mathematical verification and agent autonomy were overstated

- **PSI Research:** one advisor audited the proof and another commented on the Lean
  formalization. The PSI summary and [[AI for Mathematical Discovery]] had said two advisors
  audited. [[Automated AI Research]] no longer says formal checking let output be accepted as
  fast as it was produced; an advisor still audited the Lean-checked proof and outside experts are
  still reading it.
- **Mollick's agent teams:** a lead agent was running **28** teams by the tenth day, and
  Mollick's prompt had asked for artist, coding, and evaluator teams. [[Ethan Mollick]] had said
  the agent ran 28 teams for twelve days and organized the work itself. The entity now says he
  **sometimes** gets early model access, as his own disclosure states.
- **AI R&D acceleration:** [[Epoch AI]] says Anthropic centers its **public** case on AECI, and
  that Zvi notes METR's most relied-upon evidence is largely redacted.
- **Arete's teacher:** [[Knowledge Distillation]] no longer orders Nous's licence and benchmark
  criteria; the capture gives no sequence.
