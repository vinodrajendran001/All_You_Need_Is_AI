# AI Builder Club - Agentic Engineering: Karpathy's New Framework

## Summary

AI Builder Club interprets Andrej Karpathy’s reported Sequoia Ascent 2026 remarks as a distinction between casual “vibe coding” and professional “agentic engineering.” The latter keeps humans responsible for specifications, architecture, security, evaluation, and review while delegating larger implementation units to increasingly capable coding agents. The article argues that model improvements shift the bottleneck from producing code to directing, verifying, and refining agent work.

## Key claims

- Vibe coding broadens access to working prototypes but does not establish a quality or security bar.
- Agentic engineering requires detailed specs, architectural diff review, measurable evaluations, security oversight, and taste.
- The unit of programming is shifting from individual lines toward delegated macro-actions such as implementing features, refactoring systems, and repairing tests.
- Human understanding remains necessary even when reasoning and implementation are heavily delegated.
- Verifiable feedback loops let agents improve their work more reliably than repeated prompting without objective signals.
- Hiring should test candidates on substantial agent-assisted projects and adversarial hardening rather than only small coding puzzles.

## Why it matters

The framing recasts coding-agent use as an engineering management and verification discipline. It connects agent productivity to the quality of specifications, tests, review practices, and conceptual ownership rather than to prompt fluency alone.

## Tensions / open questions

- The source is a secondary interpretation of a talk, and its quotations and timeline should be checked against the primary recording.
- Claims of gains far beyond “10x” are directional rather than supported by a defined productivity study.
- The boundary between vibe coding and agentic engineering may be a continuum rather than a clean category split.
- Heavy human review can become the new throughput bottleneck as agent output scales.
