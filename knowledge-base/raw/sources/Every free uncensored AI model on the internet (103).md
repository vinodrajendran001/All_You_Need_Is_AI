## Quick answers

##### What is an uncensored model?

An open AI model with its refusals removed. Some are retrained on new data, others have the part that says "no" surgically cut out (you'll see "abliterated" or "heretic" in the name). It answers directly instead of hedging.

##### Why would a trader want one?

Privacy: it runs on your machine, so strategies, positions and keys never leave it. No hedging or "not financial advice" filler. No API bill and no rate limits, so it can sit inside a bot all day.

##### What do the scores mean?

They come from the public [UGI Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/DontPlanToEnd/UGI-Leaderboard). Uncensored IQ is how much it knows and how freely it answers. Reasoning is how well it thinks things through. Answers is how often it agrees to answer, out of 10.

##### What does "needs 20 GB" mean?

The model has to fit in your graphics card memory, or in a Mac's memory. Pick your computer at the top and the list shows only what fits. Every command uses the Q4 size: small, still sharp.

##### Is it safe? Can it trade for me?

Commands download from Hugging Face, the main home of open models. Any AI can be confidently wrong: test on paper first and never let it trade without hard limits. Use the models within the law and each model's licence.

##### How do I start in 2 minutes?

Install [Ollama](https://ollama.com/download) (free), pick your computer above, copy the command from the top card, paste it in Terminal. It downloads once, then you chat offline.
