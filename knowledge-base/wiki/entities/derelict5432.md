# derelict5432

A LessWrong author writing on AI risk. Present in this vault for one post.

## Why they matter to this vault

[[derelict5432 - Adaptive Agentic Worms Are Here]] brought arXiv preprint 2606.03811v1 into the vault and is
the anchor for [[Self-Replicating Agents]]. Its contribution is less the paper walkthrough than the framing
placed around it: that the decisive fact about a self-replicating agent is **economic**, because a worm
propagating on stolen compute has **zero marginal cost per infection** while defensive cost scales with assets
defended — and that this makes centralised safety controls, in the author's phrase, *"structurally
irrelevant"* to the threat.

That argument is what [[Agent Security and Governance]] had been missing. The vault's security material is
largely about containment, and containment is a per-deployment control that does not reach an adversary
running outside anyone's deployment.

## Caveats

The author **self-describes as not a security expert**, and the post is a walkthrough of a
**non-peer-reviewed preprint**. Several of the more striking framings — the structural-irrelevance claim, the
extrapolation to Darwinian population-level evolution under directed mutation — are explicitly the author's
own speculation rather than findings from the paper. The distinction is preserved wherever this source is
cited.
