# Himank Sehgal - Microsoft Applied Scientist Interview Guide

## Summary

This community guide describes a Microsoft Applied Scientist 2 process with a paper-and-coding screen followed by four loop rounds covering DSA, statistics, ML depth, and hiring-manager or behavioral discussion.

## Key claims

- The screening round combines a research-paper deep dive with coding.
- The DSA round expects progression from brute-force reasoning to an optimized implementation.
- Statistics covers fundamentals, regression, and hypothesis testing.
- ML depth connects transformers and vision-language models to the candidate's projects.
- The guide characterizes intuition and stepwise reasoning as more important than memorized coverage.

## Why it matters

It contributes a role-specific routing map to [[ML Research Interview Preparation]], especially for balancing research communication, coding, statistics, and specialized ML depth.

## Tensions / open questions

- The guide is unofficial and interview composition varies by role, level, team, and hiring cycle.
- Candidate reports do not establish universal evaluation criteria.
