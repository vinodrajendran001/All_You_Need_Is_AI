# Himank Sehgal - Google Research Scientist Interview Guide

## Summary

This community guide frames Google's research-scientist process as recruiter and technical screens followed by a loop spanning coding, ML fundamentals, domain depth, research discussion, system design, and behavioral assessment.

## Key claims

- Coding remains material even for research-centered roles.
- Research candidates should explain paper choices, experimental design, failures, and impact, not merely list publications.
- ML breadth and mathematical derivation coexist with specialization-specific depth.
- Research system design tests problem framing, data, metrics, scalability, and responsible deployment.

## Why it matters

It contributes a role-specific routing map to [[ML Research Interview Preparation]], especially for connecting a publication record to product-scale research reasoning.

## Tensions / open questions

- The guide is unofficial and interview composition varies by role, level, and team.
- Candidate reports do not establish universal evaluation criteria.
